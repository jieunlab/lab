WELCOME TO JUNG LAB

Chemistry for a
sustainable future.

Our research focuses on the development of molecular catalysts for artificial photosynthesis and sustainable energy conversion. We design transition-metal complexes and investigate their photochemical and electrochemical properties to enable efficient transformations involving CO₂, H₂O, and N₂.

Through molecular design and mechanistic studies, we aim to create catalytic systems that produce useful fuels and chemicals using light and electricity.
